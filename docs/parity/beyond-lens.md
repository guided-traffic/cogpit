# Beyond Lens

> **Target:** Lens Desktop 2026.10.51501

What Cogpit does that Lens Desktop does not, or does only in part. These rows are Cogpit's own
targets; they do not count toward the parity numbers in
[`target-app-parity.md`](target-app-parity.md), which measure Cogpit against Lens. Method:
[`README.md`](README.md).

**Summary (measured):** 77 of 78 rows exist: 66 done and 11 partial. MCP control (17 rows), the
built-in agent (10), cache transparency (2) and alerts (2) are done. Partial: the one-click update
and package-manager installs, the Helm crash offer, four standby rows, copying from the drawer, the
integrations' section and first-connect question, and `cogpit-cli get`. Missing: a hotbar tile
dragged out of the window opening another window. Open work:
[G2](gaps.md#g2-cluster-connection-and-authentication) (standby),
[G14](gaps.md#g14-packaging-signing-and-updates-macos) and [G22](gaps.md#g22-windows-and-linux)
(updates), [G3](gaps.md#g3-cache-and-the-resource-model) (cache transparency),
[G7](gaps.md#g7-resource-actions) and [G11](gaps.md#g11-helm) (crash recovery),
[G21](gaps.md#g21-built-in-agent) (the built-in agent); [G17](gaps.md#g17-mcp-control) (MCP
control) has nothing open.
